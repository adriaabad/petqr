# Demo photographs

These three fictional pet portraits were created with the built-in Image Gen tool for this project, then locally resized/re-encoded to WebP (1200×800, quality .84) without original metadata. They are bundled in the repository; no image-generation service is called by PetQR at runtime.

- `assets/pets/cooper.webp`
- `assets/pets/luna.webp`
- `assets/pets/mochi.webp`

Exact prompt template (one call per subject):

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: fictional demo pet profile photograph for PetQR. A single {subject}. Warm natural editorial pet photography, beautiful realistic fur detail, eye-level portrait, horizontal composition, centered pet with space around face for responsive cropping. No text, no graphics, no human, no watermarks, no frame. Save asset name concept {name}.

Subject values:

| name | subject |
| --- | --- |
| cooper | a friendly golden retriever, golden fur, happy relaxed open mouth, natural garden soft green background |
| luna | a tabby-and-white cat, green eyes, wearing a plain teal collar, seated on an oatmeal linen cushion, soft window light |
| mochi | a white and tan lop rabbit, long soft ears, sitting in a leafy green garden, calm natural pose |

Replace these with photographs you are authorized to publish. The images and fictional profile information are not evidence of real animals, medical conditions or ownership.
