# Photo usage

The ten current animal photos were supplied by the repository owner as Pexels downloads and are used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). They are locally resized and re-encoded as WebP. The supplied originals are unchanged.

The owner requested removal of optional photographer credits for these images. Pexels does not require attribution, so the current photo entries have no credit metadata, the website shows no photo-credit link, and exports/builds omit an empty PHOTO_CREDITS.txt file. This does not relicense the photos as MIT or public domain.

Optional credit metadata remains available for other images that need it. If a selected photo has supplied author/license information, exports preserve it and generate the corresponding credits. Source-code and dependency license notices remain separate.
